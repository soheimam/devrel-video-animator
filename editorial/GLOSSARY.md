# Glossary

Technical terms that must always be spelled exactly like this on screen. Whisper is given this list as a prompt, and `npm run validate` rejects overlays that contain a listed mis-hearing.

Format: one bullet per term, with known mis-hearings in `(avoid: …)`.

Add your product names, APIs and CLI commands here. Keep it to terms that actually appear in your videos.
